A few things to know

Who can report an item?

Any registered member can submit both lost and found reports.

Does an AI match prove ownership?

No. AI only suggests similarities. An administrator reviews private evidence before approving a claim.

Are my contact details public?

Profile contact details and report contact information are visible only to you and authorized administrators.

What if AI is unavailable?

You can still report, search, submit claims, and receive status updates normally.
