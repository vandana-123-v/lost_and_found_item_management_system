COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Terms & privacy

Submit accurate reports and claim only belongings you own. Do not publish other people's personal information in descriptions or images.

What is visible

Approved item titles, descriptions, images, general locations, dates, colors, and brand information are visible to signed-in members. Profile details, serial numbers, private identifying details, holding locations, and ownership evidence are restricted.

AI assistance

When enabled by your institution, item descriptions and images are sent to Google Gemini for analysis. Private report details and claim evidence may be sent for administrator-only consistency analysis. Do not upload government identifiers, payment card numbers, or unrelated personal documents. AI does not decide ownership.

Account and record handling

Administrators can review reports, block misuse, and retain audit records. Contact your institution's administrator to request data correction, deletion, retention information, or an alternative verification method. Closed reports may be retained for accountability.

Safe returns

Arrange handovers through authorized staff. Claims require independent human review. These guidelines are for the demonstration system; deploying institutions should publish their own privacy contact and retention policy.
