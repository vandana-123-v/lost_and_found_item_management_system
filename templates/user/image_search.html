How image matching works

{% if clip_enabled %}
  1. Your photo is encoded with a local CLIP model into a vector.
  2. The system selects up to 12 eligible found reports using category, brand and color metadata.
  3. CLIP compares your photo's vector with each found item's pre-computed image embedding.
{% else %}
  1. Gemini describes your photo.
  2. The system selects up to 12 likely found reports using category, brand and color.
  3. Gemini compares your photo directly with their photos.
{% endif %}

Optional category and location filters narrow the search. Search photos are not saved as reports. Only approved or matched found reports with images are eligible; your own reports and returned items are excluded.

Similarity scores are estimates, not probabilities or proof of ownership. A human administrator must verify any claim.
